# Solavisetech-Women-in-Data-Cohort-3
 About the Program

The SolaviseTech Women in Data (SWID) Program is a learning program designed to introduce women to the world of data and help them develop the knowledge and skills needed to explore opportunities in the field.

Through this program, I hope to learn more about data science, understand how data can be used to solve real-world problems, and gain practical experience through the different learning activities and sessions.

 My Goals

By the end of this program, I aim to:

- Build a strong foundation in data science.
- Develop practical skills for working with data.
- Learn how to use data to solve real-world problems.
- Improve my problem-solving and analytical thinking skills.
- Become more confident in working on data-related projects.
- Connect and learn from other women interested in technology and data.

 What I Hope to Achieve

My main goal is to move from being curious about data science to having a better understanding of the field and the confidence to apply what I learn. I look forward to learning, practising, making mistakes, and improving throughout the program.

I am excited to see how much I can learn and achieve by the end of Cohort 3.
